Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I need to refer people to earn in the Level Circle?

No. The Level Circle does not require referrals. Members earn through the system structure while learning how online income and affiliate systems work.

So how do I earn without referring?

Earnings come from participation in the Level Circle structure. This system is designed to reward patience, learning, and system flow, not recruitment.

Is the Level Circle a get-rich-quick scheme?

No. This is not a get-rich-quick platform. We do not promise instant wealth or guaranteed income. The Level Circle is a learning-and-earning foundation system.

Why did you remove referrals from the Level Circle?

Referrals were removed to reduce pressure, exaggeration, and spam. This allows members to learn first before promoting anything.

Will there be a referral-based affiliate program?

Yes. A separate affiliate marketing program is currently being developed. That program will involve referrals and allow members to earn in dollars.

When will the affiliate program be launched?

The affiliate program is still under development. We are taking time to build it properly to ensure transparency, security, and long-term sustainability.

Can I participate in both Level Circle and the affiliate program?

Yes. Members who complete or understand the Level Circle system will be better prepared to succeed in the affiliate program when it launches.

Is this platform legit?

This platform was built with transparency and education in mind. We explain how the system works and avoid unrealistic promises. Members are encouraged to understand everything before participating.

Who should join this platform?

This platform is for people who want to learn how online income systems work, earn patiently, and avoid scam-driven platforms.
